# Pujo by Metro 2026

**Meeting Thakur in the Metro** — a guide to Durga Puja pandals and Bonedi Bari pujas in Kolkata, organised by the Metro station you walk from.

Created entirely by **[Sayan Banerjee](https://github.com/Sayan024)**.

## What it is

- **258 pandals** (including Kolkata major pandals, Howrah & Liluah, and traditional community paras) across **39 Metro stations** on five lines, each with walking distance, exact Trishul coordinates, and verified car/bike parking.
- A line → station → pandal **explorer** with instant search, filters and sorting.
- A **Pujo map**: street map, schematic network map and a 3D overview.
- A **theme gallery**, the 2026 **puja calendar** and a day-by-day **travel advisory**.
- **My Puja List** with a day planner that orders stations, Metro rides and walks.
- An **AI assistant** that answers only from the guide's own data.
- Light and dark themes, mobile-first layout, and reduced-motion support.

Built with React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Motion, React Three Fiber, MapLibre GL and Zustand.
